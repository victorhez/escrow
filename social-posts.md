I spent 3 years on Upwork before I hit Top Rated Plus. In that time I've had exactly one recurring nightmare: doing the work, then chasing the client for payment. Late nets, "let me check with finance," full ghosting after the final delivery.

So I built the fix.

Escrow is an AI agent with its own wallet. The client funds a milestone into it. I submit the deliverable straight to the agent. # LinkedIn post

For the last few years I've built my business on Upwork — got to Top Rated Plus doing full-stack and AI engineering work. In that time, the single biggest tax on freelance income isn't taxes. It's payment friction: work that's done and sitting there while a client "reviews" it, invoices that go unanswered for weeks, disputes over whether a deliverable actually meets what was agreed.

Every freelancer I know has a story like this. So for the SERV Hackathon (AgentKit track), I built something to fix it.

Escrow is an AI agent that acts as a neutral, always-available third party between a client and a freelancer. Here's the flow:

1. Client and freelancer agree on a job broken into milestones, each with clear, written acceptance criteria.
2. The client funds a milestone into the agent's own wallet (built on Coinbase's AgentKit, running on Base).
3. The freelancer submits the deliverable directly to the agent.
4. The agent reviews the submission against the agreed criteria and either releases payment immediately, or explains precisely what's missing.
5. No manual approval step. No ghosting. Funds move the second the bar is met.

The agent takes a small fee on every release — which means its incentives are aligned with getting both sides paid and unblocked, not with dragging things out.

This isn't a trading bot or a novelty demo. It's the exact problem I deal with as a freelancer, solved with the exact tool freelancers actually need: someone (or something) trustworthy holding the money until the work is real.
